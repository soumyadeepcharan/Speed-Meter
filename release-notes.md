# Speed Meter v0.1.0: Foundation Release

Welcome to the first release of **Speed Meter**. This version sets up the app shell, the design system, and the adaptive app icon. Network speed monitoring is not included yet. It is the next step.

> **Note:** Version 0.1.0 does not measure speed yet. The main screen is currently blank.

---

## What's New

### App Foundation
- **Base app shell** built with Jetpack Compose and Material 3.
- **Edge-to-edge display** for a full-screen look.

### Adaptive Theming
- **Light and dark mode.** The app follows your system setting.
- **Wallpaper colours (Android 12 and newer).** Material You colours come from your wallpaper.
- **Matching system bars.** The status bar and navigation bar icons adjust to the theme.

### Adaptive App Icon
- **Speedometer icon** with a coloured gauge and a needle.
- **Dark mode icon.** The background and needle colours adapt to dark themes.
- **Themed icon (Android 13 and newer).** A single-colour version matches your launcher's theme.

### Compatibility and Build
- Runs on **Android 8.0 (Oreo) and newer**, on phones and tablets.
- **Optimized release builds** with R8/ProGuard minification.
- **Signed release APK** (v1, v2, v3, and v4 signatures) for verified installation.

---

## Requirements

- Android **8.0 (Oreo)** or higher
- Works on phones and tablets

---

## Installation

1. Download the APK below.
2. On your Android phone, go to **Settings → Install Unknown Apps** and allow installation from your browser or file manager.
3. Open the downloaded APK and tap **Install**.
4. Open **Speed Meter** from your app drawer.
